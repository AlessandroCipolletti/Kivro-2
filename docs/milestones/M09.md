# M09 — Availability, scheduling, queueing and future reservations

## Source, scope and sequencing

Master Spec §26, §§396–425 and §§451–487 map to exactly 150 catalog IDs. M07 remains the lease/execution authority; M08 remains the ledger/payment authority. M09 owns operational availability, schedule evaluation, admission, capacity/queue reservations and durable eligibility. Human marketplace/seller screens, public API and Agent integration are later-owned; their mapped requirements stay open until those real boundaries exist. The M07 per-job pause §§426–450 is an earlier dependency, not duplicated here.

## Pre-implementation adversarial design review

| Attack or failure | Required control |
| --- | --- |
| Seller changes schedule while checkout reserves credits | Lock one capability operational-policy row across quote revalidation, schedule update, booking and capacity count; stale quote must fail with zero reservation. |
| Many buyers take the final capacity/window slot | Serialize admission per capability and Worker, enforce bounded queue/future allocation and exact price reservation in the same transaction; only accepted jobs consume credits. |
| Immediate job bypasses a closed window | Mandatory M09 eligibility at M07 offer, acceptance and start; fail closed if policy/readiness record is missing or stale. |
| Buyer or Worker changes schedule, pause, queue limit or capacity | Only authenticated seller/platform control-plane commands may mutate operational rows; job inputs and Worker completion are never configuration authority. |
| Schedule closes after job starts | New starts stop, an already running job continues under its M07 lease; queued jobs project to the next window or expire. |
| DST skipped/repeated wall time | Evaluate seller-local membership from real UTC instants; no fixed offset or guessed clock conversion. Preserve exact UTC quote/timing evidence and document repeated-hour behavior. |
| Seller moves the next window after payment | Keep the original promise/deadline; recalculate next eligibility, append audit/event, permit pre-execution cancel, expire and release if the new time exceeds bounds. |
| Worker sleeps during a scheduled window | Fresh authenticated heartbeat and version-bound readiness remain mandatory; job waits until a usable window/Worker or maximum wait, never asserts fake online/ETA. |
| Process crashes after payment or eligibility transition | PostgreSQL transaction makes reservation and schedule state atomic; idempotent reconciliation resumes from durable rows and never duplicates ledger or events. |
| Buyer secures a job before uploading inputs, then leaves or never returns | Booking holds a bounded place and credits; M07 refuses Worker offers until a finalized immutable input manifest exists. An inputless older job cannot starve a ready buyer. Reconciliation expires the missing-input job at its original deadline and releases credits. |
| New immediate buyer cuts ahead of earlier scheduled buyer | Deterministic accepted-order arbitration per capability; earliest accepted eligible job is first unless future explicit priority policy changes. |
| Public availability leaks seller work pattern | Expose only operational state, accepting flag, next eligible timestamp and coarse explanation by default; full recurring schedule remains seller-private. |

## Implemented boundary and evidence

- `packages/contracts/src/availability.ts` and `packages/domain/src/availability-schedule.ts` define and evaluate seller-local schedules at real UTC instants, including skipped and repeated DST minutes, overnight ranges, inherited Worker defaults and bounded previews. `tests/availability-schedule.test.mjs` covers Zurich and New York DST, overnight, overlap and invalid zones.
- `packages/persistence/migrations/0016_availability.sql` persists version-bound readiness, seller/platform control state, append-only audit, immutable short-lived quotes, original job promises, mutable next eligibility and immutable lifecycle events. `packages/persistence/src/worker-heartbeat.ts` rejects stale/reordered heartbeat replay and only accepts policy-hash-bound readiness; `apps/worker/src/availability-reporter.ts` reports readiness from fresh local sandbox, secret and runtime checks. No secret or seller path enters the heartbeat.
- `packages/persistence/src/availability.ts` makes seller schedule/policy edits, public status, quote, booking, buyer cancellation, seller demand overview, M07 eligibility and restart-safe reconciliation one shared Core model. Booking holds capability and Worker locks and reserves M08 credits in the same PostgreSQL transaction as the durable schedule plan. It projects demand across capabilities sharing one Worker and rejects new quotes while changed schedules await reprojection. It preserves the original buyer deadline, recalculates `nextEligibleAt`, returns claimed pre-start work to waiting when a window closes, and releases credit on cancellation or expiry. Running work continues under M07's lease.
- `packages/application/src/scheduler-loop.ts` and `tools/kivro-scheduler.mjs` run bounded durable reconciliation. Local development uses `DATABASE_URL`, `KIVRO_STRIPE_MODE` and `KIVRO_SCHEDULER_INTERVAL_MS`; `pnpm scheduler` runs the host-native process. `--once` gives a bounded operational invocation. Provider deployment wiring remains M19.
- `tests/m09-postgres-integration.mjs` executes real PostgreSQL two-buyer final-slot racing, duplicate/reordered heartbeat, seller authorization, schedule quote replay/staleness, credit reservation/release, deterministic older-job priority across one or two capabilities, future-workload shrink, shared-Worker future and immediate capacity, immutable pinned-version execution after new publication, Worker-offline window, schedule edit, running-job survival, claim/cancel and START/expiry races, restart reconciliation, audit immutability and privacy projection. It also takes a scheduled credit reservation through M07's validated result finalization and M08's idempotent settlement, proving persisted start/delivery timestamps and exactly one seller earning. `tests/availability-reporter.test.mjs`, `tests/availability-scheduler-loop.test.mjs` and `tests/scheduled-job-metrics.test.mjs` cover the Worker, runner and timing contracts.
- The PostgreSQL test also secures a paid job before input upload, proves that no offer escapes without a manifest, allows another ready job to advance, finalizes inputs later, forbids input edits after offer, and expires a never-uploaded job with full credit release.

## Adversarial corrections to earlier milestones

M07 offer/materialization/accept/START and accepted-input release now require the M09 eligibility verifier and deny absent policy, stale readiness or a closed schedule. A pending offer made stale by a seller edit is skipped without crashing Worker polling. M07's generic transition method cannot bypass its lease-controlled execution path. M08 exposes transaction-scoped reservation, cancellation and release methods so M09 can atomically coordinate slot admission and credit truth; a buyer can cancel before STARTING even after a Worker claims a lease. Historical paid jobs retain their pinned published version and matching Worker readiness when a capability's current version changes. M09 never lets a Worker authorize payment or schedule.
M07 input finalization now accepts paid pre-dispatch scheduled/queued states, preserving purchase-before-upload. It still locks the job and denies any input edit after a Worker offer.
