## Risk and sequencing boundary

The broker may fetch public data, but an isolated M07 job cannot invoke it until Worker authentication, immutable job/version binding and secured-payment dispatch exist. Cloud/platform research egress is preferred; seller-private resources stay local. The M04 sandbox remains offline and fail closed. UI publication, buyer disclosure, OpenClaw invocation, and two-profile E2E depend on later milestones, so their exact gates remain `DEFERRED_VERIFICATION` with closing evidence in the backlog. In particular, tests of policy helpers alone do not prove a hostile model cannot exfiltrate private knowledge through a GET query. The per-job private-resource-read barrier blocks subsequent research; M07/M16 must prove the complete combined data-flow boundary.
