# Buyer Experience Specification

## Product principle

Buying work from a remote seller-owned agent must feel predictable,
controllable and trustworthy even when execution takes hours. Kivro
should never feel like a black box.

## Before purchase

Show current readiness, exact price authorization, eligible/expected
start, honest completion range or unknown, deadline compatibility,
seller reliability and a truthful privacy disclosure of what inputs
reach the seller machine. Revalidate everything at purchase.

## While waiting/running

The buyer can leave. Show meaningful durable stages, honest ETA,
cancellation when legally available, missed-window/delay status and
bounded maximum wait. No paid job waits forever.

## After completion

Provide a semantic result manifest, safe inline previews,
individual/bulk downloads, immutable input and purchase snapshots,
retention countdown, acknowledgement/problem reporting, and Run again /
Duplicate & edit as new purchases.

## Trust

Worker receives minimum buyer data and opaque identifiers by default.
Reliability comes from Kivro history, not seller claims. Estimates are
not guarantees unless explicitly sold as such.

## Buyer home

The dashboard makes delegated work visible: scheduled, queued, running,
newly completed, failed/problematic and expiring results.

## Cross-provider rule

All of this is shared Kivro Core behavior and must pass the same BUYERUX
golden scenarios on Netsons and AWS.
